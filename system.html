STRIKE SYSTEM

Three strikes. Then the rule is the rule.

A single bad trade is human. A repeat pattern is a habit. A loss past your strike line earns a strike; a win past it earns one back. The system lets you wobble, but only so far.

1
First Strike
A loss past your strike line lands the first one. COACH SAYS names it. Trading continues, but stay selective from here.
2
Second Strike
One more significant loss ends the day. Take only the cleanest setup. Most traders stop right here.
3
Third Strike
The day closes. New entries stop. You can still journal and review. Getting back in takes a deliberate override, not a click you'll make by accident.
Why three? One warning gets ignored, a single stop feels punitive. Three steps gives you the room to be human while removing the room to spiral.
