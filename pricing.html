PRICING FAQ

Questions, answered straight

If something here is still unclear, email info@maketzo.co and we read every message.

Can I get just the Edge + Pulse indicators?

Yes. The indicators alone on your TradingView charts are $29 a month or $290 a year, same invite pipeline and 24-hour turnaround, without the journal, the Focus Room or the coaching. Charged today, no trial, cancel any time. Edge + Pulse, explained.

How does MAKETZO compare to TradeZella, Tradervue or Edgewonk?

They record and analyze trades well, and MAKETZO does that too. The difference is what happens before the mistake: checkpoints you set, strikes, and a day that closes when your own rules say so. The feature-by-feature comparison is on the Journal page.

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

Yes. Card-on-file is how we keep the trial friction-free, with no awkward "convert now" interruption on day 7. We don't charge anything until day 8, and you can cancel from your account at any point before then.

What happens on day 8?

Your chosen plan's first charge runs ($29, $49, or $79) and your trial converts to a full subscription. We email you a reminder 3 days before, so day 8 is never a surprise.

Can I cancel during the trial?

Yes, any time. Canceling from your account page ends the trial and prevents any charge. No phone call. No "let me transfer you." Done.

What's your refund policy?

If we charge you and you change your mind within 7 days of that charge, email info@maketzo.co and we'll refund, no friction. Beyond 7 days post-charge, cancellation stops future billing but past charges stand.

Do I have to pay to use the tools?

Mostly no. Two run right here with no signup: the Trading OS, and Trade Replay for 10 free replays. Inside the app, Halt Clock, Focus Timer, the Trading OS, and the Knowledge Center are on every plan from day one, Journal included. One tool needs Pro: Dilution Check, which reads a company's SEC filings.

Can I switch between Journal, Pro, and Elite later?

Yes. Upgrades are immediate and prorated, so you get the new features straight away and pay the difference. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle, so you keep what you paid for.

What about annual billing?

Annual saves you 17 to 32%. Journal at $290/year, Pro at $399/year, Elite at $649/year. Same trial mechanics, and you can cancel anytime. Toggle "Annual" at the top of this page to lock in the price.

How do referral credits work?

Your personal link is in your account from the day you start your trial. Every trader who signs up through it gets an extended 14-day free trial, and when they become a paying subscriber you earn one month free on your next bill, automatically. Earn up to 12 free months a year. Manage it all from your account.

Is my trading data private?

Yes. Your journal entries, rule-break data, P&L, and behavioral signals are yours alone. We never sell your data, never hand your trades to advertisers or data brokers, and never feed your trading data to any AI model. If we ever build a feature that needs it, it stays off until you turn it on. Export it or delete it whenever you want. Full detail in our privacy policy.
