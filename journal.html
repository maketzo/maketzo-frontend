THE JOURNAL, IN FULL

Everything you'd expect. Nothing you'd settle for.

The score that grades how you traded, and everything else a serious journal needs.

Record

  • Daily journal: your plan, what worked, what didn't, and tomorrow's focus
  • Live P&L entry: type your P&L after each trade, whatever broker you use
  • DAS Trader import: your fills from DAS Trader Pro, trade by trade
  • Sticky Notes: a scratchpad you can pop out over your platform
  • Journal export: PDF, Word, OneNote, or email
  • No-Trade Day: a day you sat out, kept as a decision

Analyze

  • P&L breakdowns: by symbol, time, hold, and tag
  • Calendar & streaks: your month at a glance, saved as an image
  • Reports: equity curve, profit factor, R-ratio, and ¢/share

  • View Chart: entry and exit marked

  • Performance by Setup: which setups make you money
  • Trade Replay: trade any past chart again with a different entry or exit

Review

  • Picard Score: a grade from 1 to 10 for how you traded, not what you made
  • Rules checklist: your rules, written once and ticked off in every journal entry
  • Rule Check: mark each of your rules Followed or Broke, every day

  • My Playbook: your own notes on setups
  • Flash Cards: your playbook as cards, and the ones you miss come back sooner
  • Knowledge Center: the trading library, searchable
