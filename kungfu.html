The Five Floors

Each floor is a stretch of the trading day, and each boss is one habit. Beat it and the game hands you the fix.

  1. 9:30The Open

    Chasing

    Buying a stock because it already ran.

    The fixMissed the entry? Then you missed it. The next setup is always coming.

  2. 10:00First Pullback

    Averaging Down

    Adding to a losing trade so the average looks better.

    The fixYour stop is part of the plan. Adding to a loser is not.

  3. 10:30Mid-Morning

    Oversized

    A position bigger than your own plan allows.

    The fixYour plan sets your size. A good feeling does not.

  4. 12:00Lunch Chop

    Tilt

    Trading on emotion after a run of red trades.

    The fixThree red trades in a row? Step away before the fourth.

  5. 3:00Power Hour

    Revenge Trade

    Forcing a trade to win back a loss.

    The fixThat loss is already booked. The next trade stands on its own.

On a keyboard: the arrows walk, jump and crouch, Z punches, X kicks, Enter starts and P pauses. On a phone, turn it sideways: drag anywhere on the left to move, and tap Punch and Kick on the right.
