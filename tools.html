Trade the tape

Halt Clock

Estimate when a halted small-cap reopens, so an LULD halt never catches you flat-footed.

  • Enter the last price and the resumption price. It counts the halts the move needs, about 5% per 5-minute halt, up or down.
  • Regular hours only, when the LULD bands run. A reopen that would land after 3:50 holds to the 4:00 bell.
  • Pops out into its own window in Chrome or Edge, so it sits beside your charts.
  • An estimate, and it says so. Halts are unpredictable.
IN THE APP · FREE
Sticky Notes

A scratchpad on your MAKETZO screen, for the half-thought at 10:41 you would otherwise lose.

  • Up to 20 notes in five colors. Drag them anywhere on the screen.
  • Send to Journal copies a note into today's entry. The note stays until you clear it.
  • Synced, so a note written at the desk is on your laptop. Alt+S shows or hides them, and only you can hide them.
