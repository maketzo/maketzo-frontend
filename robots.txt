# maketzo.co — production robots policy
# Marketing site is fully crawlable. Auth/account flows are noindex (also set
# via <meta name="robots"> on those pages) and disallowed here to save crawl
# budget. The app (app.maketzo.co) has its own robots.txt (Disallow: /) and an
# X-Robots-Tag: noindex header — it is a private, auth-gated portal.
# dev.maketzo.co / staging.maketzo.co are basic-auth gated (crawlers get 401)
# and additionally send X-Robots-Tag: noindex.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /signup
Disallow: /login
Disallow: /verify-email
Disallow: /forgot-password
Disallow: /reset-password
Disallow: /unsubscribed

Sitemap: https://maketzo.co/sitemap.xml
