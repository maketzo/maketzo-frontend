EDGE + PULSE

Two questions, answered on one chart

Most losing sessions aren't bad setups. They're good setups traded at the wrong moment, or held long past the point the move was over. Edge and Pulse answer the two questions that decide which it is.

Maketzo Edge

Is the window open?

Edge marks the narrow windows when conditions line up for your setup, and just as clearly, when they don't and the play is to stand down. Once you're in, it flags the cues to scale out or step off.

Maketzo Pulse

Is there energy behind it?

Pulse reads the momentum under the move, rising or fading, at a glance. It runs on top of Edge as a second confirmation, so you hold the moves with real power and stop feeding the ones that are out of gas.
